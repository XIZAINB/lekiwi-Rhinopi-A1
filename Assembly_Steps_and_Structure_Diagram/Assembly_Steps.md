### Arm Assembly Steps：

### （手爪部分组装步骤）

![Arm](.\Images\Arm.png)

**Step 1**：

Insert the first motor horizontally into the groove of the base, and secure it with four M2×6mm screws, two on the top and two on the bottom.

(The 3D printed part constrains the orientation of the servo; only servos in the correct orientation can be assembled properly. Attach the small disc to the servo at the joint and fasten it with M3×6mm screws.)

将第一个电机横插入底座凹槽，用4个M2的6mm螺钉固定，上下各两个。

（打印部件限制了舵机朝向，所以正确朝向的舵机才能正常组装，关节连接处舵机需安装小圆盘，用M3x6mm的螺钉固定）

**Step 2**：

Slide the servo mounting bracket down from above to lock the first servo in place, then secure it with two M2×6mm screws on the left and right sides.

将固定舵机的外壳支架从上往下滑入，固定第一个电机，并用左右两个M2x6mm螺钉固定。

**Step 3**：

Mount the bus servo driver board onto the 3D printed part.

将总线舵机驱动板固定到打印材料上。

**Step 4**：

Slide the assembly with the servo driver board mounted into the groove of the housing from Step 2 to secure the driver board.

将装载好舵机驱动板的组件滑入第二步外壳的凹槽，实现驱动板的固定。

**Step 5**：

Install the F-shaped shoulder connector, assemble it with the first servo, and secure using M3×6mm screws.

安装“F”型肩部连接件，与第一个舵机组装并用M3x6mm螺钉固定。

**Step 6**：

Fit the square mounting bracket into the slot.

将方形固定支架套入卡槽位置。

**Step 7**：

Fit the second servo into the slot, and secure it with the M2 screws included in the servo kit.

(Likewise, mount the small disc on the servo at the joint and fasten it with M3×6mm bolts.)

槽位卡入第二个舵机，并用舵机套件自带的M2 螺钉固定。

（同样，关节连接处的舵机需要安装小圆盘，并使用M3x6mm的螺栓固定）

**Step 8**：

Connect the long 3D printed part with a groove to the servo of the second joint. The grooved end faces upward and is left vacant for now. Fit the other end onto the second servo for assembly and secure with M3×6mm screws.

将自带凹槽的长条打印零件与第二关节的舵机连接，有凹槽的一端朝向上方，且暂时留空，用另一端与第二个舵机嵌入组装，用M3x6mm螺钉固定。

**Step 9**：

Slide the third servo down into the groove of the support part, and secure it with M2×6mm screws on the left and right sides.

(The small disc also needs to be installed at the joint between the servo and linkage.)

将第三个舵机从上往下滑入支撑部件的凹槽位置，左右两侧用M2x6mm螺钉固定。

（舵机与关节连接处同样需要安装小圆盘）

**Step 10**：

Connect the fork end of the shorter elongated part to the third servo, with the slot on the other end facing upward.

较短的长条形部件叉口与第三个舵机连接，另一端槽位朝上。

**Step 11**：

Fit the housing mounting bracket into the slot to form a complete groove.

将固定外壳支架套入卡槽中，形成一个完整的凹槽。

**Step 12**：

Fit the fourth servo into the slot and secure it with M2 screws.

将第四个舵机装入卡槽中，并用M2螺钉固定。

**Step 13**：

Connect the small joint part with a built-in servo groove to the fourth servo, and secure with M2×6mm screws.

自带舵机凹槽的小型关节件与第四个舵机连接，用M2x6mm螺钉固定。

**Step 14**：

Insert the fifth servo into the slot with the rotary-disc end first. Mount the rotary disc on the side facing outwards toward the opening; do not fit the small disc on the other side.

将第五个舵机以带圆转盘的一端插入卡槽内，朝孔隙外的一侧安装圆转盘，另一侧不用安装小圆盘。

**Step 15**：

Assemble the gripper fixture with the fifth servo and secure it with M3 screws.

夹爪固定件与第五个舵机组装，并用M3螺钉固定。

**Step 16**：

Fit the sixth servo into the slot of the gripper fixture. Connect the holes on both sides of the housing to the servo using M2×6mm screws. Also mount the small disc on the servo gear with M3×6mm screws.

将第六个舵机卡入夹爪固定件的槽位，外壳两侧的孔位与舵机用M2x6mm的螺钉相连，舵机齿轮处同样需要都用M3x6mm的螺钉装上小圆盘。

**Step 17**：

Connect the structural part that controls the rotation of the other gripper jaw to the rotating disc of the sixth servo.

将控制另一只爪子转动的结构件与第六个舵机转动圆盘处连接。

**Step 18**：

Fit the soft gripper 3D printed part in place, and fasten the jaw with M3×20mm bolts and nuts.

装上软夹爪打印件，用M3x20mm左右的螺栓和螺母固定爪子。

**Step 19**：

Assemble the other soft gripper in the same way, and fasten the jaw with M3×20mm bolts and nuts.

同样组装软夹爪，用M3x20mm左右的螺栓和螺母固定爪子。

### Assemble Wheel Modules (3 per robot)

### （组装车轮底盘）

**Step 1**：

Attach the drive motor to the motor mount using 4 m2x5 tap screws.

使用 4 颗 M2×5 自攻螺钉，将驱动电机固定在电机座上。

![IMG_9193 (1)](.\Images\IMG_919.jpg)

**Step 2**：

Attach the wheel hub to the omniwheel using 2 m4x12 machine screws.

使用 2 颗 M4×12 螺钉，将轮毂固定在全向轮上。

![DSC04](.\Images\DSC04.jpg)

**Step 3**：

Attach the caster wheel to the servo disc using 4 M3×16 machine screws.

用 4 个 M3x16 的螺丝将万向轮固定到舵机圆盘上。

![DSC03](D:\桌面\robohub\LeKiwi\Assembly_Steps_and_Structure_Diagram\Images\DSC03.jpg)

**Step 4**：

Fasten all three wheels to the chassis.

将三个车轮全部固定到底盘上。

![DSC07](.\Images\DSC07.jpg)

### Power and Driver Board Assembly

### （电源及驱动板的组装）

Secure the two 12 V 5 A lithium battery packs to the base plate with cable ties. One pack supplies power to the chassis and manipulator, while the other powers the RhinoPi A1. These lithium battery packs come with built-in protection boards, delivering stable output and matching the 12 V 3 A power requirement of the RhinoPi A1, so no additional step-down voltage regulator is needed. In addition, connect the serial bus driver board to the servo wiring harness, and fasten it to the base plate using printed components and screws.

将两块 12V 5A 锂电池组用扎带固定到底板上，一块给底盘和机械臂供电，另一块给犀牛派 A1 供电，采用的锂电池组内置保护板，能稳定输出，且匹配犀牛派 A1 的 12V3A 供电需求，因此不再添置降压稳压器；另外将串口总线驱动板与舵机线束连接，并通过打印材料与螺钉固定到底板上。

![IMG_170641](.\Images\IMG_170641.jpg)

### Cable Management

### （线束整理）

Route the wiring harness from the first servo of the upper manipulator, the power cable for RhinoPi A1, and the USB data cable together through the reserved opening on the 3D‑printed top base plate.

上方机械臂第一节舵机引出的线束、犀牛派A1板卡供电的电源线缆、USB数据线缆一起穿过3D打印上底板的预留孔径

![IMG_1930](.\Images\IMG_1930.jpg)

### LeKiwi Assembly

（组装LeKiwi）

Fasten the upper and lower chassis together. Mount the RhinoPi A1 and robotic arm onto the upper chassis using bolts and nuts. Attach the camera to the gripper of the robotic arm via 3D-printed parts. Connect all wiring harnesses, and the LeKiwi assembly is complete.

将上下底盘进行固定，将犀牛派A1和机械臂通过螺栓螺母固定到上底盘，摄像头通过3D打印材料固定到机械臂夹爪上，连接好所有的线束，LeKiwi就完成制作了。
